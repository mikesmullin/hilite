hilite

single-file DOM inspector — point, and the agent knows what you mean press E

Try it

Press E to arm the inspector, then hover anything below for a reference card, click to cite one element, or click+drag to lasso a region. The citation lands on your clipboard and the marked elements keep a .hilite class on the DOM for the agent to find. Press Esc to disarm — the marks stay, hidden, until you re-arm.

Why is the latency spike on the agentic-chat route?
Assistant

The spike correlates with the model list fetch: each turn re-queries /models instead of reusing the cached manifest, and the endpoint round-trips to the gateway on a cold cache.

The fix is a per-session manifest with a 30 s TTL. P50 drops from 412ms to 96ms in the local harness.

A source

manifest.ts — the session manifest, cached per conversation: 

const manifest = await fetch("/models", { cache: "force-cache" })
  .then((r) => r.json());
session.set("models", manifest.models, TTL_30S);
